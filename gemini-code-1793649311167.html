🚀 How to Install & Launch on Mac

  1. Locate the downloaded AI Council.zip file in your Downloads folder.
  2. Double-click AI Council.zip to extract the AI Council app.
  3. Drag the extracted AI Council app into your Applications folder.
  4. Double-click AI Council to open and start using the multi-agent workspace!
Connecting to your local AI model: Make sure your local LM Studio server is running on your Mac (default endpoint: http://localhost:1234) so your agent team can send and receive responses.
